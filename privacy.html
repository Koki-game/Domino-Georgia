DOMINO GEORGIA

Privacy Policy · Updated 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DOMINO GEORGIA handles information when you use the game website, installed web app and online multiplayer features.

1. Information used by the game

  • Google sign-in information: Firebase Authentication may receive an account identifier, email address, display name and profile photo from Google for authentication.
  • Game profile: in-game nickname, country/flag, level, hearts, wins, games, virtual points, nickname-change count, owned cosmetic items and selected table style.
  • Online match data: room code, seat, connection/presence information, game state, match events and temporary player identifiers needed to synchronize multiplayer games.
  • Local device data: language, profile cache, room/reconnect state and other settings may be stored in browser/app storage.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate players, maintain game profiles and virtual progress, synchronize online matches, reconnect players to active games, show in-game identity such as nickname/flag/level, and operate or secure the service.

3. Google account data

Your Google account is used for sign-in. Your Google display name may be suggested when choosing a nickname, but players are intended to see your in-game nickname rather than your Google email address. Deleting a DOMINO GEORGIA account does not delete the user's Google account.

4. Service providers

DOMINO GEORGIA uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide authentication and multiplayer/profile storage. Those services process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing, advertising and purchases

We do not sell personal information. The current version does not use third-party advertising SDKs. The cosmetic shop currently uses only virtual game points and does not process real-money payments.

6. Storage and retention

Account/profile information is retained while the account is active. Online room data is used to operate matches and may remain temporarily until rooms are cleaned up. Local settings remain on the device until cleared, the app/site data is reset, or account deletion clears game storage.

7. Account and data deletion

Users can request and complete deletion from inside the game or from the web deletion page below. The deletion flow removes the DOMINO GEORGIA Firebase Authentication account, game profile, virtual progress/cosmetics, and the user's player entry from active online rooms where found.

Delete DOMINO GEORGIA account

8. Security

Firebase authentication and Firestore access controls are used to help protect account and game data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children and age requirements

Players should use the service only when permitted by applicable laws and platform age requirements. DOMINO GEORGIA does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the game profile.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the game changes. The update date shown above will be revised when material changes are made.

11. Contact

A permanent public support contact will be published on the Google Play listing before public release. During testing, users can use the support channel through which they received access to the game.

If analytics, advertising, payments, new social features or additional data collection are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form must be updated before release of those features.
