DOMINO GEORGIA

Privacy Policy · Updated 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DOMINO GEORGIA handles information when you use the game website and its online multiplayer features.

1. Information we use

  • Google sign-in information: when you choose Google Sign-In, Google/Firebase Authentication may provide an account identifier, email address, display name and profile photo.
  • Game profile: your chosen in-game nickname, country/flag selection, level, hearts, wins and related game progress.
  • Online match data: room identifiers, seat position, connection/presence status, game state and match events needed to synchronize multiplayer games.
  • Technical data: Firebase and your browser/device may process technical information needed for authentication, security and service operation.

2. How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, keep your game profile, synchronize online matches, reconnect you to an active game, show player identity such as nickname/country/level, and protect the service from misuse.

3. Google data

Your Google account is used for authentication. Your Google display name is only a suggestion for the in-game nickname; you may choose a different nickname. Other players are intended to see your in-game nickname and game profile information, not your Google email address.

4. Firebase / service providers

DOMINO GEORGIA uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. These services process data according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. We do not currently use third-party advertising SDKs in the game. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase when required to operate the game.

6. Storage and retention

Profile and game data may remain stored while the account/service is active or as reasonably needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the game. Some local settings are stored in your browser/device.

7. Children and age requirements

Players should use the service only when permitted by applicable laws and platform age requirements. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the game profile.

8. Security

We use Firebase authentication and access controls to help protect account and game data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You can sign out of Google from the game lobby. Before public Google Play release, DOMINO GEORGIA will also provide the account/data deletion flow required for released apps with account creation.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game changes. The update date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy or support requests during the current testing phase, use the support channel through which you received access to DOMINO GEORGIA. A permanent public support contact will be published before the Google Play release.

This policy describes the current testing version. It should be reviewed again immediately before Google Play publication, especially if analytics, ads, purchases, chat moderation, or new account features are added.
